Hardware Architecture & Wiring
Component ESP32 GPIO Notes
RC522 RFID SDA GPIO 5 SPI Chip Select (SS)
RC522 RFID SCK GPIO 18 SPI Clock
RC522 RFID MOSI GPIO 23 SPI Master Out
RC522 RFID MISO GPIO 19 SPI Master In
RC522 RFID RST GPIO 4 Hardware Reset
HX711 Load Cell DT GPIO 32 Serial Data Output
HX711 Load Cell SCK GPIO 33 Clock Pulse

Servo Motor PWM GPIO 13 External Regulated 5V
Single LED GPIO 25 Dedicated Indication LED
Safety & Circuit Rules:
• Use ONLY the authorized components listed. Do not connect ultrasonic or extra actuators.
• Power the Servo motor from a dedicated regulated 5V supply with common ground with ESP32.
• Connect RC522 VCC to 3.3V, NOT 5V.
