RFID Smart Card Scan

Continuous ATM-style scanner instantly identifies citizen entitlements, family cards, and monthly allocation with salted 6-digit PIN verification.

HX711 Precision Load Cell

Automated servo gate dispensing paired with dual-stage weight validation prevents under-weighing and eliminates diversion of essential commodities.

Cloud Firestore Real-Time

Every single gram dispensed is synchronized live with Cloud Firestore across all devices. Permanent audit trail ensures complete transparency.

Citizen Self-Service Portal

Citizens can tap their card to view monthly quota balances, transaction timestamps, and download receipts in their secure 5-minute session.
